Atlas Daily Reports

Privacy policy

Updated 25 October 2026.

Purpose and Google access

This private application sends daily reports to its account owner. It requests Gmail permission to send email and Google identity access to verify the authorized email address. It does not request permission to read the Gmail mailbox, contacts, calendar or Drive.

Data use and storage

Google OAuth client credentials and a refresh token are stored in private files on the reporting server, accessible only to the reporting operating-system account and server administrators. Access tokens are used to call Google over HTTPS. Report files contain simulated paper-trading assessments and ledger-integrity results. They are stored privately on the server and sent as email attachments to the owner. Delivery records store report identifiers, timestamps, recipient address and Gmail message identifiers. Credentials are not intentionally included in reports or application logs.

Sharing and retention

Email content and authorization data are transmitted to Google to provide sign-in and Gmail delivery. The server provider hosts the private application and its files. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. Reports and delivery records remain on the private server until removed by the owner; sent email remains subject to the owner's Gmail settings. Credentials are retained until access is revoked or the application is decommissioned.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke this application's access in Google Account third-party connections, stop the reporting schedule, and remove report files, credentials and delivery records from the private server. Removing server credentials alone does not revoke Google's authorization grant.

Atlas Daily Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For non-sensitive support, use the app support page. Do not post reports, passwords, tokens or private account information there.
